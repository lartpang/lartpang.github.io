I’m Youwei Pang (庞有伟), a Research Fellow at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Weisi Lin.

Before that I spent ten years at Dalian University of Technology: a B.E. in Electronic Information Engineering (2015–2019), then a Ph.D. in Signal and Information Processing (2019–2025), advised by Prof. Lihe Zhang and Prof. Huchuan Lu.

Most of my projects are built together with my best friend Xiaoqi Zhao.

Research

My long-term goal is visual intelligence that generalizes across scales, tasks and modalities, stays efficient from large models down to real deployments, and earns trust through reliable, diagnostic evaluation — so that what a model perceives can be measured, compared and relied on.
