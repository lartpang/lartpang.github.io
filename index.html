Research Fellow · CCDS, Nanyang Technological University

My long-term goal is visual intelligence that generalizes across scales, tasks and modalities, stays efficient from large models down to real deployments, and earns trust through reliable, diagnostic evaluation — so that what a model perceives can be measured, compared and relied on.

01

Generalizable

  • Multi-scale perception — mixed-scale, high-res, tiny targets
  • Unified multi-task — context-dependent concepts
  • Multimodal — RGB-D/T, video, open-vocabulary, missing modality
02

Efficient

  • Architectures — efficient attention, dynamic operators
  • Deployment — large model feature coding, lithium battery & semiconductor inspection
03

Reliable

  • Evaluation — metric decomposition, diagnosis, toolkits
  • Benchmarks — X-ray inspection, feature coding, agents
  • Content safety — harmful content
